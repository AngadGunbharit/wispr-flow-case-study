Why this matters

It's the happiest segment's main job

In my scan of 124 public reviews, 11 of 12 people who dictate AI prompts were positive and none were negative. No Wispr page speaks to them.

Longer prompts get better answers

Speaking makes a detailed prompt cheap. Cleanup that trims it gives back the time voice just saved.

It's the first step to voice-to-action

A prompt is already an instruction. Flow structuring it, then sending it to the right tool, is how dictation becomes action.

What I'd measure: edit and undo rates on dictations in AI apps with Prompt Mode on vs. off, and week-4 retention for users whose top app is an AI chat. The message cleanup shown here is a simulation for comparison, not Wispr's model.
